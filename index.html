PERFORMANCE_LAB Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 22, 2026

In short: PERFORMANCE_LAB keeps your training data on your device. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. Two optional features involve other services: Health Connect (off until you turn it on) and the one-time Pro unlock purchase. Both are explained below.

What the app stores on your device

  • Your workout log: the exercises and variants you log, repetitions or hold times, and the time of each set.
  • Your progress and skill data, and your settings, including your reminder preferences.
  • Backup files the app saves on your device. You can also export a backup file or import one. When you export, you choose where it goes through your device's share sheet; I never receive it.

This data is stored locally and is not sent to me or to any server I run. I do not run a server for this app.

Health Connect (optional, Android)

Health Connect sync is off by default. If you turn it on in the app, the app asks for permission to write exercise sessions to Health Connect on your device. It does not read any health data from Health Connect.

  • What is written: for each set you log, one exercise session containing the exercise name and variant, the repetition count or hold duration, and start and end times (the duration is estimated from the set).
  • Where it goes: into Health Connect on your own device. It is not sent to me, to a server I run, or to any third party by the app.
  • Use: Health Connect data is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not shared with anyone.
  • Control: turn the sync off in the app, undo a set to remove the record it wrote, or manage and delete the data and revoke the permission in Health Connect settings.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules reminders locally on your device. It does not use a push notification service and does not collect a notification token.

Purchases (optional Pro unlock)

The app offers an optional one-time Pro unlock. Payment is handled by Google Play. To confirm that you own the unlock (and to restore it on a new device), the app uses RevenueCat, a purchase-management service. RevenueCat receives purchase and entitlement information needed for that check. It does not receive your workout log or any Health Connect data. RevenueCat's handling of that information is described in its own policy at revenuecat.com/privacy, and Google's at policies.google.com/privacy.

What the app does not collect

  • No account, name, email address, phone number, location or contacts.
  • No advertising ID, analytics, crash reporting or tracking of any kind.
  • No advertising and no third-party advertising or analytics code.

Sharing

I do not sell your data or share it with anyone, apart from the purchase check with Google Play and RevenueCat described above.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your training data stays on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app. Health Connect records stay in Health Connect until you delete them there or undo the set in the app. Purchase records are held by Google Play and RevenueCat under their own policies; contact me if you want help with a request to them.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and it does not collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
